The project incorporates three different utilities to create an accessbile environment. The first acommodation our group included are translations for many popular languages. Individuals primarily speaking a language other than language will feel comfortable using our product. Next, the chatbot assists individuals who find typing difficult by allowing users to click icons of champions to formulate their team. We understand that typing team compositions quickly might be diffcult for some people, so the use of the icons allows everyone to choose the best champions possible. Finally, our team implemented the chatbot to remember the past 30 conversations a user has had with the AI. We hope that this feature assists people with poor memories and those who find using technology difficult.
